# The Watch (bettersecurity.ai): working notes for Claude

Jerome Jayapal's multi city cybersecurity events calendar, jobs board and Sunday email, published by Better Security. Cloudflare Pages Functions plus D1 (`thewatch`), a scheduled Worker in `workers/feed-cron`, Resend for email. Push to main deploys. The long form history and decisions live in the Better Security project docs (claude/1 to claude/173); the operations runbook is doc 170, the incident postmortem is doc 169, the DR plan and risk register are doc 171.

## Standing rules (do not relax these)
- Never sign in anywhere on Jerome's behalf. Never send email, publish a newsletter, post or message anywhere without his approval. Never click Apply, connect or message on LinkedIn.
- Jobs: names only from public posts; links only to the employer's own posting, never an aggregator. Events: the organizer's own registration page, never an aggregator or reseller.
- Roles people post about (decided 2026-10-23, widened the same evening): when someone who works at the employer posts publicly about a role (a hiring manager, a security leader, an in-house recruiter, or someone on that team or a sister team, `posted_by_role` `team`), the role is shown with that person's name, title and a link to their post (never copied post text), labeled by who they are. Their profile must show they currently work at the employer. Staffing agency posts stay out, and so do people who only post and repost other companies' roles to grow their network, career coaches, and university staff posting for another employer. Remote roles open anywhere in the US go on the remote board (`city` `remote`, /remote/jobs), and each city board shows them in a section of their own. Every role announced in such a post is listed, whether or not the employer has posted it yet: one not yet on the employer's own site is listed as announced, linking to the post, and switches to the employer's posting once it appears. These roles publish immediately, with no 24 hour hold, because speed is the point. They come from targeted searches in Jerome's own LinkedIn and from matching posts to roles already on our boards, never from scraping. Anyone named can ask to be removed.
- Member only events are listed, not rejected: set `members_only=1` (shown as a Members only tag) and say who can attend in `cost_notes`. Invitation only and application only events (Evanta summits and Inner Circle dinners, vendor CISO dinners) are listed too (decided 2026-10-23): leave `members_only=0`, state the access in `cost_notes`, and do not hold them for that reason alone.
- Publish by default with 24 hour holds. New rows land as `pending_review` with `auto_publish_at`; hold (`hold=1`) only for a stated reason written in `reviewer_notes`.
- Writing: professional and friendly, flowing paragraphs, no dashes or ellipses, no exclamation marks, never "genuinely", "honestly", "actually" or "charm". Emails sign off "Thank you,".
- Secrets live in the Cloudflare dashboard only. Never paste them into chat, commits or docs. The repo is public.
- Holds in force: featured role price, sponsor conversations, Padel Social and the dinner series, organizer outreach, LinkedIn API and page posting, the contact campaign.

## Conventions
- Commits: say what and why; end with the co-author line for the model that wrote it and the session link.
- Schema changes are numbered files in `migrations/`, applied by hand to production (D1 connector or `wrangler d1 migrations apply thewatch --remote`) and to staging (D1 connector on database `f4236033-993f-4427-945b-e7b9eaa4777d`, or `wrangler d1 migrations apply thewatch-staging --remote --env preview`), then committed. Preview deployments read staging, never production; see `docs/environments.md`.
- Bulk data goes through `functions/admin/seeds.js`: idempotent statements in `functions/_lib/seeds/<name>.js`, indexed by `index.js`, generated by `tools/city-launch/seed.py`. Guards must use indexed columns (`slug`, `registration_url`, `lower(title)`, `start_at`, `lower(organizations.name)`, `feed_sources.url`).
- Cities are data (`cities` table plus `tools/city-launch/gen.py` CITIES). Launching a city is research plus a seed, not code.
- Check a deploy: `gh api repos/jayapalhub/thewatch/commits/<sha>/check-runs --jq '.check_runs[0] | "\(.status) \(.conclusion)"'`.
- Before and after any bulk load, look at `/api/health` (usage_today). Daily read budget is 50 million rows.
- Public pages are edge cached for 5 minutes with a 2 day stale fallback (`functions/_middleware.js`). Anything personal or admin is in the PRIVATE list there and never cached.

## Where things are
- `functions/_lib/events.js` listing queries; `feeds.js` feed adapters; `job-sources.js` ATS adapters; `dispatch.js` newsletter; `autopublish.js`; `ops.js` metering, health, alerts; `casestudy.js` the gated case study content.
- `functions/admin/*` admin pages behind Cloudflare Access. `functions/api/*` JSON endpoints; the cron Worker calls `/api/events/autopublish`, `/api/feeds/pull` and `/api/newsletter/tick` with the ADMIN_TOKEN.
- `tools/city-launch/` research to seed tooling and the playbook.
