# The Watch (bettersecurity.ai): working notes for Claude

Jerome Jayapal's San Diego security and technology events calendar, Virtual calendar and Sunday email, published by Better Security. The Watch is the lead generation for Better Security's events business: small, sponsored, invite only experiences for San Diego security and IT practitioners starting in 2027, so the site also collects invitation requests. Cloudflare Pages Functions plus D1 (`thewatch`), a scheduled Worker in `workers/feed-cron`, Resend for email. Push to main deploys. The long form history and decisions live in the Better Security project docs (claude/1 onward); the operations runbook is doc 170, the incident postmortem is doc 169, the DR plan and risk register are doc 171, the consolidation to San Diego is doc 222.

## Consolidated to San Diego (2026-10-25)
- Only San Diego is live. The other 39 cities are paused (`cities.active=0`), never deleted: their events, organizers, issues and history stay in the database, and a city comes back by setting `active=1` in /admin/cities and reactivating its `feed_sources` and `sweep_targets` rows (each paused row's notes say "paused 2026-10-25, city hidden"). Paused city paths redirect to /sandiego (`_middleware.js`), appear nowhere in navigation or the sitemap, and get no sourcing, refresh or newsletter.
- The job board is gone from the public site, the sitemap, the Sunday email, the hourly tick and the LinkedIn schedule. The `jobs`, `job_sources` and related tables stay as an archive (all `job_sources` paused), readable at /admin/jobs. Do not add job pages, job sourcing or job links back without Jerome asking.
- The Virtual calendar, its sourcing, the San Diego | Virtual toggle and its block in the newsletter are unchanged.
- Jerome's own events stay off public pages: never name specific events, dates, venues, sponsors, the Padel Social or ISACA on a public page. Say "experiences", not "dinners". Never describe the audience as GRC and audit; it is technology and security broadly.

## Standing rules (do not relax these)
- Never sign in anywhere on Jerome's behalf. Never send email, publish a newsletter, post or message anywhere without his approval. Never click Apply, connect or message on LinkedIn.
- Events: the organizer's own registration page, never an aggregator or reseller.
- The job board rules (roles people post about, trust and safety roles) are retired with the board; they are in the git history and docs 199 to 203 if the board ever returns.
- Member only events are listed, not rejected: set `members_only=1` (shown as a Members only tag) and say who can attend in `cost_notes`. Invitation only and application only events (Evanta summits and Inner Circle dinners, vendor CISO dinners) are listed too (decided 2026-10-23): leave `members_only=0`, state the access in `cost_notes`, and do not hold them for that reason alone.
- Publish by default with 24 hour holds. New rows land as `pending_review` with `auto_publish_at`; hold (`hold=1`) only for a stated reason written in `reviewer_notes`.
- Writing: professional and friendly, flowing paragraphs, no dashes or ellipses, no exclamation marks, never "genuinely", "honestly", "actually" or "charm". Emails sign off "Thank you,".
- Secrets live in the Cloudflare dashboard only. Never paste them into chat, commits or docs. The repo is public.
- Holds in force: sponsor conversations, Padel Social and the experience series, organizer outreach, LinkedIn API and page posting, the contact campaign.

## Conventions
- Commits: say what and why; end with the co-author line for the model that wrote it and the session link.
- Schema changes are numbered files in `migrations/`, applied by hand to production (D1 connector or `wrangler d1 migrations apply thewatch --remote`) and to staging (D1 connector on database `f4236033-993f-4427-945b-e7b9eaa4777d`, or `wrangler d1 migrations apply thewatch-staging --remote --env preview`), then committed. Preview deployments read staging, never production; see `docs/environments.md`.
- Bulk data goes through `functions/admin/seeds.js`: idempotent statements in `functions/_lib/seeds/<name>.js`, indexed by `index.js`, generated by `tools/city-launch/seed.py`. Guards must use indexed columns (`slug`, `registration_url`, `lower(title)`, `start_at`, `lower(organizations.name)`, `feed_sources.url`).
- Cities are data (`cities` table plus `tools/city-launch/gen.py` CITIES). Launching or reviving a city is research plus a seed and an `active=1` flag, not code.
- Check a deploy: `gh api repos/jayapalhub/thewatch/commits/<sha>/check-runs --jq '.check_runs[0] | "\(.status) \(.conclusion)"'`.
- Before and after any bulk load, look at `/api/health` (usage_today). Daily read budget is 50 million rows.
- Public pages are edge cached for 5 minutes with a 2 day stale fallback (`functions/_middleware.js`). Anything personal or admin is in the PRIVATE list there and never cached.

## Where things are
- `functions/_lib/events.js` listing queries; `feeds.js` feed adapters; `dispatch.js` newsletter; `autopublish.js`; `ops.js` metering, health, alerts; `casestudy.js` the gated case study content. `job-sources.js`, `jobs.js`, `coverage.js` and `employers.js` serve the admin archive only.
- `functions/admin/*` admin pages behind Cloudflare Access. `functions/api/*` JSON endpoints; the cron Worker calls `/api/events/autopublish`, `/api/feeds/pull` and `/api/newsletter/tick` with the ADMIN_TOKEN.
- `tools/city-launch/` research to seed tooling and the playbook.
