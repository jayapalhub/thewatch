# 161. City launch playbook, addendum (2026-10-22)

Read with doc 154, the city launch playbook. These changes came out of the ten city launch in doc 160 and replace step 3's many D1 calls with one seed per city.

- **Tooling lives in the repo** at tools/city-launch: BRIEF.md (the research brief and JSON shapes both agents write), gen.py (research JSON to idempotent SQL: city row, organizations with default venues, feeds, deduped sweep targets, events with automatic hold rules, boards including second-city copies of existing employers) and seed.py (writes the statements as functions/_lib/seeds/<city>.js and rebuilds the index). Set CITY_DATA to the folder holding <slug>_events*.json and <slug>_boards.json.
- **Loading is one POST.** Commit the seed, wait for the deploy, then from a tab on bettersecurity.ai POST /admin/seeds with name=<slug>. Statements come only from the bundle and are idempotent, so a follow-up research file (<slug>_events_2.json) is folded in by regenerating and re-running. This replaces the many D1 calls of steps 3.1 to 3.5.
- **Budgets.** WebSearch allows about 200 calls per main-session turn shared by every agent launched in that turn, and WebFetch has an hourly cap shared across agents. Launch at most two to four agents per turn with an explicit search allowance in the brief (45 to 70 each), and schedule follow-up passes after the fetch cap resets. Research agents run on Sonnet.
- **ISACA calendars** (engage.isaca.org) cannot be read by the research tools. From a Chrome tab on engage.isaca.org, a same-origin fetch of /<chapter>/events/calendar and of /events/eventdescription?CalendarEventKey=<key> reads every chapter's list and the event location in a few calls.
- **Adapters.** Hosted iCIMS portals (careers-<name>.icims.com) use the icims_classic kind, which the generator picks for any icims.com host; Lockheed's TalentBrew site does not work with our adapters and is skipped. Lever boards on jobs.eu.lever.co need {"region":"eu"}. Workday site names recalled from memory usually return 422: pause with the note and let the sweep or a later pass find the real site.
- **Feeds right after a new city row** are safe now: the importer refreshes the city list before using a new city's time zone (it used to fall back to Pacific within the first minute).
- **Feed hygiene.** Hackerspace and maker groups need title-only keywords from the first pull, and feed rows often duplicate researched rows with slightly different titles; reject the feed copy, which stays rejected by its feed uid.
